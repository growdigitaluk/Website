Our story

Why Grow Digital exists

Grow Digital helps small businesses in Manchester and across the UK with three things: websites, AI onboarding and social media. We started it because a lot of small business owners are told they need an expensive agency or complicated software, when what they actually need is simpler — a clear website, sensible use of AI, and someone reliable who answers their messages.

Grow Digital is led by its founder, Luqman. Before starting the business, Luqman spent more than 12 years in UK financial services, working in quality assurance, compliance and financial crime. That work is about getting the details right, explaining rules in plain English and handling information carefully.

Those habits carry straight over. They're why we publish our prices, put quotes in writing, register domains in our clients' names, and treat data protection as part of setting up any AI tool — not an afterthought.

Who you'll work with

Luqman leads every project, so you deal with the same person from the first call to launch and beyond. When a project needs specialist skills — for example photography, branding or more advanced development — we bring in trusted specialists we know. You'll always know who's involved, and Luqman stays your point of contact.
