About Grow Digital

AI, websites and social media for UK small businesses. We help firms use AI safely, look good online and stay consistent on social media — with one team on every project.

Our story

Why Grow Digital exists

Grow Digital helps UK small businesses and professional firms with three things: AI onboarding, websites and social media. AI comes first because it's where small firms can save the most time — and where getting it wrong with client data matters most. Too many owners are told they need an expensive agency or complicated software, when what they actually need is simpler: sensible use of AI, a clear website, and a team that answers their messages.

Grow Digital was founded by a compliance professional with 12+ years in UK financial services (quality assurance, compliance, financial crime). That's why we take data safety seriously: that work is about getting the details right, explaining rules in plain English and handling client information carefully.

Those habits carry straight over. They're why we publish our prices, put quotes in writing, register domains in our clients' names, and treat data protection as part of setting up any AI tool — not an afterthought.

Who you'll work with

Our team leads every project, so you have one point of contact from the first call to launch and beyond. When a project needs specialist skills — for example photography, branding or more advanced development — we bring in trusted specialists we know. You'll always know who's involved.
