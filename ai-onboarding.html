Our approach

Careful with data from day one

Luqman spent over 12 years in UK financial services working in quality assurance, compliance and financial crime. That background shapes how we approach AI: useful, but with sensible controls.

We help you think through data protection in practical terms, but we're not lawyers and this isn't legal advice. If your business is regulated, we'll work alongside your compliance lead or your regulator's guidance.

What that means in practice

  • We start by understanding what information your business handles.
  • We recommend business plans and settings that stop your data being used to train AI models, where the tool allows it.
  • We agree clear red lines — for example, no client personal data, passwords or bank details in tools that aren't approved for it.
  • AI drafts, people decide: every output that matters is checked by a person.
  • We work in your accounts. We don't copy your business data into ours.

FAQ

AI onboarding questions

Which AI tool should my business use?

It depends on how you work. If your business runs on Microsoft 365, Copilot may fit best. ChatGPT and Claude are strong all-rounders. We'll recommend one based on your tasks, budget and data needs — not on what's fashionable.

Do I need to pay for an AI subscription?

For business use, a paid plan is usually worth it for the extra privacy controls and features. Subscription costs are paid directly by you to the provider and aren't included in our prices.

Is it safe to use AI with client information?

Only with the right tool, plan and settings — and clear rules. Part of every package is agreeing what can and can't go into AI tools, so your team isn't guessing.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can write an email, you can use these tools. We go at your pace and leave you with a cheat sheet to refer back to.

Is the training online or in person?

Sessions run online by video, so we can work with businesses anywhere in the UK. In-person sessions can be arranged.

Will AI replace my staff?

That's not what we set it up for. The aim is to take some of the repetitive writing and admin off your plate, so you and your team have more time for the work that needs a person.
