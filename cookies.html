Cookie policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so you won't see a cookie banner. We don't track you across other websites.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device. Some are needed for a site to work; others are used to track visitors or show adverts. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels.

Services this website connects to

  • Google Fonts — our fonts are loaded from Google's servers. Your browser shares your IP address with Google to download them. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
  • Formspree — when you send a message using our contact form, it is processed by Formspree. Formspree may use strictly necessary technology to prevent spam when you submit the form.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook — if you click a link to these services, you leave our website and their own cookie policies apply.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we'll update this page and ask for your consent before setting them, as UK law requires.

Managing cookies

You can view and delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. For more information, visit the ICO's guidance on cookies at ico.org.uk.
