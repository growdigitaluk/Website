FAQ

Common questions

How much do your services cost?

Websites start at £349, AI onboarding at £149, and social media management at £249 a month. Every package and price is on our pricing page, and you'll get a fixed quote in writing before any work starts.

Can I use more than one service?

Yes. You can start with one and add others later — for example a new website first, then social media that sends people to it. We'll only suggest what's actually useful for your business.

Where are you based, and who do you work with?

We work with small businesses across the UK. Almost everything runs smoothly by phone, video and email, so it doesn't matter where you are.

Will I own my website and accounts?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and paid for by you, and your website content, AI accounts and social media accounts all belong to you.

Is it safe to use AI tools with my business information?

It can be, with the right settings and some clear rules. We set up each tool's data and privacy controls, help you choose the right plan for business use, and write a simple safe-use policy so your team knows what should and shouldn't go into an AI tool.

Do you need my social media passwords?

No. You add us as a partner through Meta Business Suite, so you stay in control and can remove our access at any time.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime with 30 days' notice. Websites and AI training sessions are one-off fixed prices.

What happens on the free intro call?

It's a relaxed 20-minute chat by phone or video. We'll ask about your business, answer your questions and suggest what would actually help. If it's a good fit, we'll follow up with a written quote.
