FAQ

Pricing questions

Are there any hidden costs?

No. Your written quote lists everything. The only extras are things paid directly to other companies — your domain (at cost), any paid software subscriptions, and advertising if you choose to run ads.

How do payments work?

For website projects, we take a 50% deposit to book your project and the remaining 50% before launch. AI sessions are paid before the session. Monthly plans are paid monthly in advance.

Can you build something that isn't listed?

Probably. Tell us what you need on a free call and we'll give you an honest answer and a fixed quote. If it's outside what we do, we'll say so.

How does the founding client offer work?

Our first 10 clients get 15% off any website or AI package. In return, we ask for an honest review once the work is done — good or bad, it's your genuine opinion we want.
