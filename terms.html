1. Quotes

We give every client a written quote setting out the work, price and expected timeline. Quotes are valid for 30 days. Work starts once you accept the quote and any deposit has been paid.

2. Prices

Our prices are listed on our pricing page and confirmed in your quote. Prices exclude ad spend and third-party subscriptions — for example domain names, paid AI plans, advertising, or paid plugins — which you pay directly to the provider.

3. Founding client offer

Our first 10 clients receive 15% off any website or AI package, in exchange for an honest review once the work is done. The discount applies to one-off website and AI packages, not monthly plans, and to the first project only. We ask for your genuine opinion; you are under no obligation to leave a positive review.

4. Payment

  • Websites: a 50% deposit to book your project, and the remaining 50% before the website goes live.
  • AI Kickstart and AI Team Workshop: paid in full before the session.
  • Monthly plans (care plans, AI Support and social media): paid monthly in advance.

Invoices are due within 7 days unless your quote says otherwise. We may pause work or monthly services while an invoice is overdue.

5. Your part

To keep projects on schedule, we'll need you to send content (such as text, logos and photos), give feedback and approve work in good time. You confirm that you own, or have permission to use, everything you send us. Timelines are estimates and depend on receiving content and feedback when needed.

6. Revisions

Each website package includes a set number of revision rounds (2 for Starter, 3 for Business, or as stated in your quote). A round is one set of feedback sent together. Additional changes, or changes to the agreed scope, may be quoted separately.

7. Domains and hosting

Domain names are registered in your name and paid for by you at cost. Hosting is included in our Care and Care Plus plans. If you don't take a care plan, you are responsible for your own hosting once the website is handed over.

8. Monthly plans and cancellation

Care plans, AI Support and social media plans run month to month. You can cancel anytime by giving 30 days' notice by email. If you cancel a care plan, we'll help you move your website to another host.

9. Ownership

Once you've paid in full, you own your website's design and content, and any content we create for you. We keep ownership of our general tools, code and templates, which we may reuse. With your permission, we may show your project on our website.
